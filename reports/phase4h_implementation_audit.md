## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 4H-A performs an exhaustive audit of the existing codebase, model architectures, expert demonstration pipelines, reinforcement learning configurations, simulation contracts, and evaluation harnesses.

### Critical Audit Findings
1. **The 200-Step vs. 500-Step Horizon Discrepancy (Root Cause of 0.0% Success in Phase 4G):**
   - In `core/geometry.py` and `sim/corridor_env.py`, the canonical 10m benchmark corridor is configured with `max_steps = 500` ($dt = 0.05\text{ s} \implies 25.0\text{ s}$).
   - The APF expert demonstration dataset (`data/demonstrations/bc_train_audit.json`) records an average flight time of **398.2 steps** ($19.91\text{ s}$) to navigate around the 3 pillars and reach the goal.
   - In a completely obstacle-free clear corridor, the trained BC policy takes **344 steps** ($17.2\text{ s}$) and original PPO takes **379 steps** ($18.95\text{ s}$) to reach the goal within the required terminal speed limit ($v \le 0.35\text{ m/s}$).
   - Phase 4G copied a diagnostic probe cutoff (`steps < 200`, $10.0\text{ s}$) intended only for passing Obstacle 1 at $X = 3.0\text{ m}$. At 200 steps, a drone constrained to the physical speed limit ($v_x \le 0.60\text{ m/s}$) can travel a maximum theoretical distance of $6.0\text{ m}$, making reaching the 9.0m goal **physically impossible**.
   - Under the canonical 500-step horizon, both original BC and original PPO achieve **100% success** on clear corridors and offset obstacles.
2. **The Centered Obstacle Hypothesis for BC (Confirmed):**
   - When an obstacle is offset to the left ($Y_{\text{obs}} = -0.25, -0.15\text{ m}$) or right ($Y_{\text{obs}} = +0.25\text{ m}$), BC bypasses it cleanly and reaches terminal goal success ($100\%$ success in 500 steps).
   - When the obstacle is centered on the corridor axis ($Y_{\text{obs}} = 0.00\text{ m}$), BC commands near-zero lateral velocity ($v_y \approx 0.003\text{ m/s}$), flies directly toward the obstacle, and is permanently stopped by the safety supervisor at $X = 2.81\text{ m}$ (0% success).
   - Root Cause: In `controllers/apf_expert.py`, the APF expert circulates right for $Y_{\text{obs}} \ge 0$ and left for $Y_{\text{obs}} < 0$. Continuous random sampling in `generate_demonstrations.py` never produced an exact centered obstacle. Near $Y=0$, conflicting left-evasion and right-evasion demonstrations caused supervised MSE regression to average the actions to $v_y \approx 0.0\text{ m/s}$.
3. **The Unilateral Bias Hypothesis for PPO (Confirmed):**
   - PPO converged to a rightward-only evasion policy ($v_y \approx -0.25\text{ m/s}$). When the obstacle is offset to the right ($Y_{\text{obs}} \ge +0.15\text{ m}$), PPO easily passes and reaches the goal ($100\%$ success). When the obstacle is offset to the left ($Y_{\text{obs}} \le 0.00\text{ m}$), PPO refuses to take the left gap, entering an irrecoverable supervisor deadlock against the right wall/pillar buffer.

## 2. Component-by-Component Implementation Audit

### 2.1 Behavior Cloning (BC) Pipeline
- **Policy Network:** `NavigationPolicyMLP` in `models/policy_net.py`.
  - Architecture: 23 inputs $\to$ `Linear(23, 128)` $\to$ `LayerNorm` $\to$ `GELU` $\to$ `Linear(128, 128)` $\to$ `LayerNorm` $\to$ `GELU` $\to$ `Linear(128, 64)` $\to$ `LayerNorm` $\to$ `GELU` $\to$ `Linear(64, 4)`. Total parameters: 28,740.
  - Action Head: Hyperbolic tangent enforcing physical bounds:
    $\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{a}_{\text{center}} + \mathbf{a}_{\text{half\_range}} \odot \tanh(\mathbf{z})$
    $v_x \in [-0.2, 0.8]\text{ m/s}$, $v_y \in [-0.6, 0.6]\text{ m/s}$, $v_z \in [-0.4, 0.4]\text{ m/s}$, $\dot{\psi} \in [-0.6, 0.6]\text{ rad/s}$.
- **Dataset Format:** `.npz` archive containing:
  - `observations`: `(N, 23)` float32 array.
  - `actions`: `(N, 4)` float32 array.
  - `episode_starts`: `(N,)` bool array.
  - `episode_ids`: `(N,)` int32 array.
- **Dataset Statistics (`data/demonstrations/bc_train_10m.npz`):**
  - 120 accepted episodes, 47,780 transitions.
  - Mean steps per episode: 398.2 ($19.91\text{ s}$).
  - All episodes generated by `ScriptedAPFExpertController`.
- **Training Objective:** Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss with AdamW optimizer ($\text{lr}=10^{-3}$, cosine annealing to $10^{-5}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, 25 epochs).
- **Inference Latency:** Mean $0.18\text{ ms}$ on standard CPU (well below 3.0 ms requirement).

### 2.2 Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) Pipeline
- **Actor-Critic Network:** `ActorCriticPPO` in `models/actor_critic.py`.
  - Actor: `NavigationPolicyMLP(23, 4)`.
  - Critic: `ValueNetworkMLP(23)` (3-layer MLP predicting scalar state value $V(s)$).
  - Action Distribution: Continuous diagonal Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu(s), \sigma^2)$ where $\sigma = \exp(\text{clamp}(\log \sigma, -2.5, 0.2))$. Default $\log \sigma = -1.2$.
- **Training Framework:** `models/train_ppo.py`.
  - Rollout buffer: 2048 environment steps per iteration.
  - GAE advantage estimation: $\gamma = 0.99$, $\lambda = 0.95$.
  - Surrogate clipping: $\epsilon = 0.20$.
  - Optimization: AdamW, $\text{lr} = 3 \times 10^{-4}$, 4 epochs, minibatch size 128.
  - Observation presented to buffer: Raw deployable observation (or virtual observation in reflection mode).
  - Action stored in buffer: Sampled continuous action in policy coordinate frame.
  - Safety Supervisor: Active in training rollouts. Actions are filtered before simulator stepping.

### 2.3 Observation Space Contract (23 Dimensions)
Verified in `core/contracts.py`:
- `0..11`: 12-channel rangefinder distances normalized by $d_{\text{max}} = 4.0\text{ m} \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
  - Horizontal rays: $0^\circ$ (Ray 0), $+30^\circ$ (Ray 1), $-30^\circ$ (Ray 2), $+60^\circ$ (Ray 3), $-60^\circ$ (Ray 4), $+90^\circ$ (Ray 5), $-90^\circ$ (Ray 6), $180^\circ$ (Ray 7).
  - Vertical rays: Pitch $+45^\circ$ (Ray 8), Pitch $-45^\circ$ (Ray 9), Zenith $+90^\circ$ (Ray 10), Nadir $-90^\circ$ (Ray 11).
- `12..14`: Relative goal unit vector in body FLU frame $[g_x, g_y, g_z]$.
- `15..17`: Estimated body velocity in body FLU frame $[v_x, v_y, v_z]\text{ m/s}$.
- `18..21`: Attitude trigonometric vector $[\sin(\phi), \cos(\phi), \sin(\theta), \cos(\theta)]$.
- `22`: Yaw angular rate $\dot{\psi}\text{ rad/s}$.

### 2.4 Action Space Contract (4 Dimensions)
High-level continuous velocity setpoints in body FLU frame:
- $v_x$: Forward speed setpoint (range: $[-0.20, +0.60]\text{ m/s}$ in action limits, policy head $[-0.20, +0.80]$).
- $v_y$: Lateral speed setpoint (+Left, -Right, range: $[-0.40, +0.40]\text{ m/s}$ in action limits, policy head $[-0.60, +0.60]$).
- $v_z$: Vertical speed setpoint (+Up, -Down, range: $[-0.30, +0.30]\text{ m/s}$ in action limits, policy head $[-0.40, +0.40]$).
- $\dot{\psi}$: Yaw angular rate setpoint (+CCW, -CW, range: $[-0.50, +0.50]\text{ rad/s}$ in action limits, policy head $[-0.60, +0.60]$).

### 2.5 Safety Supervisor Architecture (`controllers/safety_supervisor.py`)
- Multi-directional safety filtering against walls, floor, ceiling, and obstacles.
- Forward obstacle caution buffer: $d_{\text{caution}} = 0.80\text{ m}$. When front clearance $< 0.80\text{ m}$, forward velocity is throttled proportionally down to $0.05\text{ m/s}$.
- Emergency obstacle brake buffer: $d_{\text{crit}} = 0.30\text{ m}$. Forward velocity is commanded to zero or reverse.
- Wall boundary buffer: $0.25\text{ m}$. Inward lateral velocity is capped or repelled.
- Ceiling/floor buffer: $0.20\text{ m}$. Vertical velocity is clamped.

### 2.6 Goal Position, Arrival Speed, and Termination Conditions
Verified in `core/geometry.py`:
- Benchmark Corridor: $X \in [0.0, 10.0]\text{ m}$, $Y \in [-1.0, 1.0]\text{ m}$, $Z \in [0.0, 2.0]\text{ m}$.
- Start Position: $[0.5, 0.0, 1.0]\text{ m}$.
- Goal Position: $[9.5, 0.0, 1.0]\text{ m}$.
- Goal Acceptance Sphere: Radius $r_{\text{goal}} = 0.35\text{ m}$ (distance $\le 0.35\text{ m}$).
- Terminal Arrival Speed Constraint: $v_{\text{terminal}} \le 0.35\text{ m/s}$.
  If the drone enters the goal sphere with speed $> 0.35\text{ m/s}$, the episode continues without triggering success.
- Crash Conditions: Obstacle clearance $\le 0.00\text{ m}$ or wall clearance $\le 0.00\text{ m}$ (analytical boundary check).
- Episode Timeout: Step count $\ge \text{max\_steps}$ (500 steps in canonical environment).
