## 2. Origin & Anatomy of the 14 Hard-Negative Seeds

### 2.1 Discovery in Milestone 4
During Milestone 4, the unshielded PPO policy was evaluated across 25 deterministic environment seeds (`seeds 200–224`). While early claims suggested a 72% success rate, audit logging revealed that the agent was colliding with obstacles at high frequency.

When strict physical boundary collision detection ($r_{\text{uav}} = 0.08$m) was enforced, exactly **14 out of 25 seeds resulted in direct high-speed collisions**:
```python
TRAINING_HARD_NEGATIVE_SEEDS = [
    200, 203, 204, 206, 207, 210, 213, 215, 216, 217, 218, 220, 221, 224
]
```

### 2.2 Geometric Characteristics of the Crash Seeds
Inspection of the obstacle coordinates generated by these seeds reveals three recurring geometric patterns that defeated unshielded reactive policies:
1. **Dead-Center Pillar Placement ($x \in [4.0, 7.0]\text{m}, y \in [-0.05, 0.05]\text{m}$):**
   Seen in Seeds 200, 206, 215. The obstacle is placed precisely on the corridor centerline, producing symmetric lidar returns.
2. **Squeeze Choke Points ($|\Delta y_{\text{gap}}| < 0.35\text{m}$):**
   Seen in Seeds 203, 210, 220. Two staggered obstacles force an immediate S-turn maneuver where clearance on either side is $<0.15$m.
3. **Corner Pinch & Acute Wall Proximity ($|y_{\text{obs}}| \approx 0.85\text{m}$):**
   Seen in Seeds 213, 224. An obstacle placed adjacent to the corridor boundary creates a narrow gap between the obstacle and the wall.

## 3. The PPO Sampling Contamination & Policy Collapse

### 3.1 Code Inspection: `models/train_ppo.py:36`
Forensic analysis of the PPO training script reveals the exact mechanism of failure:

```python
# From models/train_ppo.py (Milestone 4 / Phase 4B)
if np.random.rand() < 0.60:
    # 60% probability of sampling from the 14 crash seeds
    env_seed = np.random.choice(TRAINING_HARD_NEGATIVE_SEEDS)
else:
    # 40% probability of sampling random seed
    env_seed = np.random.randint(0, 1000)
```

### 3.2 Algorithmic Cause of Collapse
1. **Gradient Domination:** By forcing 60% of all rollouts through 14 highly constrained, high-penalty geometries, the PPO policy gradient was overwhelmed by terminal collision penalties ($-50.0$) and high lateral velocity variance.
2. **Mode Collapse to Unilateral Right Drift:**
   Because several early hard-negative seeds (e.g., 200, 204) had open clearance to the right, the neural policy discovered a degenerate local minimum: **command a constant, hard rightward velocity ($v_y \approx -0.23$ m/s)** immediately upon takeoff.
3. **The Supervised Failure:**
   When evaluated without the safety supervisor, this policy achieved a superficial 50% success on right-biased maps while crashing violently into left-biased obstacles. When evaluated *with* the safety supervisor, the supervisor constantly intervened to stop the drone from smashing into the right corridor wall, resulting in **12.0% (3/25) success** and 88% timeouts.

### 4.2 Forensic Diagnosis of Phase 4K
